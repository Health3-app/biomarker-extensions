## Detailed Description

Reading about blood tests online but don't know what "hs-CRP" or "DHEAS" actually means? This extension turns any webpage into an interactive health reference.

Health3 Biomarker Explainer lets you highlight any biomarker name on any webpage — patient portals, health articles, Reddit posts, research papers — and instantly see a clear explanation with reference ranges and related health topics.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Highlight a biomarker name on any webpage (e.g., "Creatinine", "Vitamin D 30 ng/mL")
2. Right-click and select "Explain with Health3"
3. An inline tooltip appears with:
   • What the biomarker is and why it matters
   • All available reference ranges, labeled by sex, age, and source
   • Related health topics
   • If you highlighted a value too, it's displayed prominently

Or click the extension icon to search and browse all 182 biomarkers directly.

WHERE IT WORKS
• Patient portals (MyChart, Quest, LabCorp results pages)
• Health articles and medical websites
• Reddit threads (r/bloodwork, r/supplements, r/biohackers)
• Research papers and clinical guidelines
• Anywhere biomarker names appear on the web

182 BIOMARKERS COVERED
Complete blood count, metabolic panel, lipid panel, liver enzymes, thyroid hormones, sex hormones, iron studies, vitamins (A, B1-B12, C, D, E, K), inflammation markers, tumor markers, trace minerals, and more.

REFERENCE RANGES — TRANSPARENT, NOT PERSONALIZED
Every biomarker shows all available reference ranges from clinical sources, clearly labeled with the criteria they apply to:
• Sex: Male, Female, or All
• Age range: specific brackets or all ages
• Source: publication name and year

We never classify your value as "normal" or "abnormal" — because that depends on your individual profile. We show the data; you and your doctor make the interpretation.

Sources include Tietz Textbook of Laboratory Medicine (2023), StatPearls, Mosby's Diagnostic Reference, and peer-reviewed studies.

POPUP SEARCH
Click the extension icon to open a searchable biomarker library. Browse all 182 biomarkers with full descriptions, reference ranges, supported measurement units, and related health topics.

PRIVACY & SECURITY
• No data is collected or transmitted
• No account required
• No tracking, analytics, or browsing history access
• The extension only activates when you right-click selected text
• All data is bundled locally — no API calls

PERMISSIONS EXPLAINED
• "contextMenus" — adds the "Explain with Health3" right-click option
• "activeTab" — reads your text selection on the current tab only when you click the menu item
• "scripting" — injects the tooltip on the page when you use the context menu
